# Swynex Task 3: Healthcare Patient and Readmission Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built on the cleaned healthcare dataset (`swynex_healthcare_hospital_cleaned.csv`, 850 rows and 16 columns) to highlight billing patterns, monthly patient trends, readmission rates, and extended stay outliers.

## Dashboard Components
- KPI Cards: Total Patients (850), Avg Billing ($60.9K), Avg Stay Days (4.82), Readmission Rate % (22.59%), and Avg Satisfaction (3.77).
- Charts: Avg Billing by medical_condition, Total Patients by Month, Readmission Rate % by medical_condition, and Length of Stay vs. Billing Amount.
- Interactive Filters: medical_condition and readmission_flag slicers.

## Key Findings
- Pneumonia and Cardiac Care record higher average billing than other medical conditions.
- Patient admissions fluctuate across months, with clear peaks and dips.
- Readmission rate stands at 22.59%, varying across medical conditions.
- Extended stays above 20 days appear as distinct outliers in the stay duration vs. billing view.
